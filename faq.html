Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our services and project process.

What services do you offer?

We offer website design and development, Android app development, website + app projects, basic SEO, Google Ads setup, social media marketing, landing page design and website maintenance.

Are the listed prices fixed?

Prices shown on the website are starting prices. Final pricing can vary based on requirements, scope, features and deliverables.

Do you offer custom projects?

Yes. You can contact us with your requirements and we can discuss a suitable scope.

How do I get started?

Use the Contact Us page and send your name, email, preferred service and project details.

Do you use a shopping cart or online checkout?

No. This website is designed for service inquiries. Customers contact us to discuss their requirements rather than adding services to a cart.

What payment terms apply?

Payment terms and any applicable milestones are agreed as part of the project arrangement before work begins.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Website maintenance and other ongoing services are available where agreed as a separate service.
